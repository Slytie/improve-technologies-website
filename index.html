INTERNAL RESEARCH NOTE · 16 SEPTEMBER 2026

Evidence changed
the next attempt.

A bounded, three-attempt control simulation shows the feedback loop operating.

  1. 01

    Propose.

    An initial candidate changed the strategy. Its results were recorded before the next research request.

  2. 02

    Test.

    A broader change exposed a regression in a separate test condition. That negative finding became part of the next attempt’s scientific context.

  3. 03

    Revise.

    The next hypothesis restricted where the change applied. The retained report records that the earlier regression disappeared.

What this establishes

The retained internal report describes evidence-conditioned hypothesis revision. Overall task success stayed unchanged. This is not a customer result or proof of general productivity gains.

The report also leaves a separate mechanism-validation gate unresolved. The live run was not re-executed for this website.
